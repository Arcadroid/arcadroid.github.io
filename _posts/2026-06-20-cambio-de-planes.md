Hey everyone, quick update on where things are at with Dust Punk.

Around mid-June, we had to take a step back and look at the project as a whole. A few circumstances came up that made us seriously reconsider the direction we were heading in.

Long story short: we ended up scrapping the original vehicle combat concept. The muscle car and tank setup was fun to mess around with early on, but it wasn't really clicking with what we actually wanted to make.

So, we pivoted. Dust Punk is now an adventure game focused on a protagonist exploring the wasteland on foot and solving puzzles along the way. It gives us a lot more room to build atmosphere, tell a proper story, and make the pacing feel right.

It’s definitely a big shift, but it feels like the right call for the game. We'll share more as things come together.
